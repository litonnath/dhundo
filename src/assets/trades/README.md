# Real photos for trades

Put a photo here named after the picture key and rebuild (`./deploy.sh`).
It replaces the drawing for that trade on the home tiles, the category
tiles, the listing cards and the sign-up list.

Landscape, about 640x400, under 100 KB. Use your own photos, or ones whose
licence allows it (Pexels and Unsplash photos are free to use).
